For **Wish Catcher**, a narrative game about immigrant experiences, I managed player communities and produced platform-specific social content for X, TikTok, Instagram, and Xiaohongshu. The work included playtest announcements, community game nights, Steam wishlist campaigns, and culturally grounded explainers that adapted the game's satire and storytelling for different audiences.

This page collects self-hosted captures of those posts so the work stays visible without relying on social platform embeds.
